# Definition of Integrals 

## What is an Integral?
The integral is the inverse operation of the derivative. While the derivative describes the instantaneous rate of change of a function, the integral does the opposite: it **reconstructs the accumulated total** from small variations.

### Geometric Interpretation
*   **Derivative:** The slope of the tangent line to the curve.
*   **Integral:** The **area under the curve** within an interval.

### Physical Interpretation
If the derivative of position is velocity, then:
*   **Derivative:** Finds the velocity at a specific instant.
*   **Integral:** Sums all the small variations in position over time to find the **total distance traveled**.

> [!TIP]
> 
> **Core Concept**
> The derivative focuses on the **instant**. The integral focuses on the **whole**.
