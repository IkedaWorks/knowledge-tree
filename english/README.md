#  English Learning Roadmap

This repository centralizes my English language studies, applying the **CLIL** (*Content and Integrated Language Learning*) framework to bridge the gap between language proficiency and **Computer Engineering** concepts.
### 🎯 Study Strategy 

* **`Shadowing & Listening:`** Active practice using *How I Met Your Mother* to master natural intonation, rhythm, and common phrasal verbs. 
* **`The Feynman Technique:`** Explaining complex engineering and physics topics in English to solidify technical vocabulary and fluency. 
* **`Full Immersion:`** Consuming technical documentation, Calculus, and Physics resources exclusively in English to build academic stamina.
