## Overview

This submodule addresses instantaneous rates of change and the geometry of curves. The derivative is the essential mathematical tool for analyzing the dynamic behavior of functions, enabling the calculation of instantaneous velocities, rates of change, tangent line slopes, and system optimization.

## Module Topics

* **Foundations and Definition:** The derivative as the limit of a difference quotient, geometric interpretation (tangent line), and instantaneous rate of change.
* **Operational Differentiation Rules:** Constant rule, power rule, sum rule, product rule, and quotient rule.
* **Chain Rule and Implicit Differentiation:** Differentiation of composite functions, implicit differentiation, and higher-order derivatives.
* **Elementary Functions:** Derivatives of trigonometric, exponential, logarithmic, and inverse trigonometric functions.
* **Applications and Optimization:** Mean Value Theorem (MVT), L'Hôpital's Rule, concavity analysis, inflection points, related rates, and maximum/minimum problems.

## Prerequisites

- **Limits and Continuity:** Mastery of limit operations, solving indeterminate forms, and the concept of continuity.
- **Analytic Geometry:** Line equations, slope (gradient), secant lines, and tangent lines.
- **Trigonometry and Functions:** Operational properties and behavior of transcendental functions.
