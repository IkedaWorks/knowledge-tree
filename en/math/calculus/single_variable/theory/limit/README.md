## Overview

This submodule is dedicated to studying the local behavior of functions and establishing the foundation for the concept of continuity. Limits serve as the fundamental framework upon which all Differential and Integral Calculus is built.

## Module Topics

* **Foundations and Definition**
  * Intuitive definition of limits
  * Formal $(\varepsilon, \delta)$ definition
  * Limit verification and interpretation

* **Properties and Theorems**
  * Operational limit properties
  * Squeeze Theorem
  * Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT)

* **Calculus Techniques**
  * Resolving indeterminate forms via factoring and simplification
  * Fundamental limits (trigonometric and exponential)
  * Variable substitution in limits

* **Asymptotic Behavior**
  * One-sided limits
  * Limits at infinity and infinite limits
  * Vertical and horizontal asymptote analysis

* **Continuity and Consolidation**
  * Pointwise and interval continuity
  * Types of discontinuities
  * Comprehensive review of the module

## Prerequisites

- **Algebra and Manipulation:** Polynomial factoring, special products, algebraic fraction simplification, and rationalization.
- **Function Analysis:** Domain, range, asymptotic behavior, function composition, and graphical analysis.
- **Trigonometry:** Unit circle, fundamental identities, and the behavior of $\sin(x)$, $\cos(x)$, and $\tan(x)$ functions.
- **Exponentials and Logarithms:** Power properties, natural logarithm ($\ln$), and Euler's constant ($e$).
