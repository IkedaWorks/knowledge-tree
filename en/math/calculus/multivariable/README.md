# Multivariable Calculus

> Extending single-variable calculus concepts to functions of several variables and vector fields in higher-dimensional space.

---

## Overview

This submodule is dedicated to the conceptual, mathematical, and practical exploration of Multivariable Calculus (commonly referred to as Calculus 3). The objective is to build a solid bridge between 3D spatial geometry, partial differentiation, and multiple integration, providing the rigorous foundation needed for theoretical physics, engineering, optimization, and machine learning.

---

## Module Topics

### Spatial Geometry and Vectors
* Vectors in R2 and R3, dot product, cross product, and triple products
* Equations of lines, planes, and quadric surfaces
* Vector-valued functions, space curves parameterization, velocity, acceleration, and curvature

### Differential Calculus of Several Variables
* Limits and continuity for multivariable functions
* Partial derivatives, differentiability, and tangent planes
* Chain Rule, directional derivatives, and the gradient vector
* Unconstrained optimization, critical points, Second Derivative Test, and Lagrange Multipliers

### Integral Calculus of Several Variables
* Double integrals over rectangular and general regions
* Change of variables in double integrals using Polar Coordinates
* Triple integrals in Cartesian, Cylindrical, and Spherical coordinates
* Applications of multiple integrals: area, volume, mass, and center of mass
* The Jacobian matrix in coordinate transformations

### Vector Calculus
* Vector fields, divergence, and curl
* Line integrals and the Fundamental Theorem for Line Integrals
* Path independence and conservative vector fields
* Green's Theorem in the plane
* Parameterized surfaces and surface integrals
* Divergence Theorem (Gauss's Theorem) and Stokes' Theorem

---

## Prerequisites

* **Single-Variable Calculus:** Proficiency in limits, differentiation techniques, chain rule, and integration methods (substitution and integration by parts).
* **Linear Algebra and Analytical Geometry:** Familiarity with matrices, determinants, linear systems, and fundamental vector operations.
