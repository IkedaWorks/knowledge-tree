## Overview

This submodule addresses instantaneous rates of change and the geometry of curves. The derivative is the essential mathematical tool for analyzing the dynamic behavior of functions, enabling the calculation of rates of change, tangent line slopes, and system optimization.

## Module Topics

* **Foundations and Definition**
  * The derivative as the limit of a difference quotient
  * Geometric interpretation (tangent and secant lines)
  * Instantaneous rate of change

* **Operational Differentiation Rules**
  * Constant, power, and sum rules
  * Product rule and quotient rule
  * Higher-order derivatives

* **Composition and Advanced Differentiation**
  * Chain Rule
  * Implicit differentiation
  * Logarithmic differentiation

* **Elementary Functions**
  * Derivatives of trigonometric and inverse trigonometric functions
  * Derivatives of exponential and logarithmic functions

* **Applications and Optimization**
  * Mean Value Theorem (MVT) and Rolle's Theorem
  * L'Hôpital's Rule for indeterminate forms
  * Function analysis (monotonicity, concavity, and inflection points)
  * Optimization problems (maxima/minima) and related rates

## Prerequisites

- **Limits and Continuity:** Mastery of limit operations, solving indeterminate forms, and the concept of continuity.
- **Analytic Geometry:** Line equations, slope (gradient), and curve behavior.
- **Trigonometry and Functions:** Operational properties and behavior of transcendental functions.
