# Financial Mathematics

> "Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world. He who understands it, earns it; he who doesn't, pays it."  
> — **Albert Einstein**

The **Financial Mathematics** module explores algebraic and arithmetic methods applied to analyzing the time value of money. It provides tools for evaluating percentage variations, interest accumulation, and capital growth in financial contexts.

This module covers fundamental percentages, percentage increases and discounts, sequential changes, as well as the core operational differences between simple and compound interest models.
