# Single-Variable Calculus

> "If I have seen further it is by standing on the shoulders of Giants."  
> — **Isaac Newton**

The **Single-Variable Calculus** module explores rates of change, asymptotic behaviors, and accumulation of continuous real-valued functions. It establishes the foundational mathematical core for quantitative fields, physics, and engineering.

This module covers limit theory, differential calculus techniques and applications, integral theory structured around the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, and power series approximations.
