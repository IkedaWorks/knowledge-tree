# Differential Equations

> "Laws of nature are written in the language of differential equations." — Isaac Newton

## Overview

Differential Equations form the backbone of mathematical modeling across physics, engineering, and applied sciences. This field is dedicated to studying equations that relate an unknown function to its derivatives, representing the rate of change of quantities with respect to one or more independent variables. Mastering this discipline enables the analysis of dynamic systems ranging from electrical circuits and mechanical oscillations to heat diffusion and electromagnetic fields.
