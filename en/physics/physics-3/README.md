## Overview

This submodule addresses the conceptual, mathematical, and intuitive exploration of Electromagnetism. The content traces the evolution from static electrostatic and magnetostatic phenomena to the complete unification of dynamic fields, culminating in Maxwell's Equations and electromagnetic wave propagation.

## Module Topics

* **Electrostatics (Static Electric Fields)**
  * Electric charge, Coulomb's Law, and force between point charges
  * Electric field ($\vec{E}$), continuous charge distributions ($dq$), and vector integration
  * Electric flux, Gauss's Law, and field calculations using spatial symmetry

* **Electric Potential, Energy, and Dielectrics**
  * Electric potential ($V$), potential difference, and field gradient ($\vec{E} = -\nabla V$)
  * Capacitance, energy storage in electric fields, and capacitors
  * Dielectric materials and microscopic polarization

* **Magnetostatics (Static Magnetic Fields)**
  * Magnetic field ($\vec{B}$), Lorentz Force, and Gauss's Law for Magnetism ($\oint \vec{B} \cdot d\vec{A} = 0$)
  * Sources of magnetic fields and the Biot-Savart Law ($I d\vec{s}$)
  * Ampère's Law and magnetic field calculations via symmetry

* **Dynamic Electromagnetism and Maxwell's Equations**
  * Faraday-Lenz Law, electromagnetic induction, and electromotive force (EMF)
  * Maxwell's displacement current and the Ampère-Maxwell equation
  * Synthesis of Maxwell's Equations and electromagnetic wave propagation in vacuum and media

## Prerequisites

- **Vector / Multivariable Calculus (Calculus III):** Proficiency in line, surface, and volume integrals, as well as differential operators (gradient, divergence, and curl).
- **Analytic Geometry and Vectors:** Vector decomposition, dot and cross products, and utilization of cylindrical and spherical coordinate systems.
- **Classical Mechanics (Physics I):** Concepts of work, potential energy, and equations of motion under applied forces.
