# Electromagnetism

> "The special theory of relativity owes its origin to Maxwell's equations of the electromagnetic field."
> — Albert Einstein

**Electromagnetism** is the unified theory describing electric and magnetic interactions. This module covers everything from electrostatic principles and continuous charge distributions to electric potential, dielectrics, and the fundamentals of magnetic fields.
