# Electromagnetism

> "The special theory of relativity owes its origin to Maxwell's equations of the electromagnetic field."
> — Albert Einstein

**Electromagnetism** is the unified theory describing electric and magnetic interactions. This module covers electrostatics, continuous charge distributions, electric potential, dielectrics, capacitance, and introductory magnetostatics.
